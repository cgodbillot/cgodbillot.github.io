Last week I once again had the great pleasure to participate in the Village des Sciences de l'Arbois, as part of the national  :tada::test_tube: Fête de la Science :test_tube::tada:. Every year, for a day, hundreds of schoolchildren invade the Technopole de l'Arbois, where tens of workshops are ready to teach them (the fun way) about Earth and Environmental Sciences. For the fourth year, I had a lot of fun showing these young scientists how fossils buried deep inside the mud can testify for a changing climate. By far my favorite (and messiest) day of the year :)
